Reflection

My first idea was Mood Fish, where a fish would react differently to the cursor depending on a selected emotion. After testing the idea, I felt that choosing moods with buttons was not very engaging, and it was also too close to my group project. I changed the project into Night Garden, a slower exploration experience. I tested the flashlight interaction, random object locations, and the scroll-wheel beam control. I found that the first forest path looked too much like a vertical light beam, so I changed it into a textured dirt path. I also lowered the success sound because it felt too loud compared with the calm atmosphere.

AI helped me organize the project into HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and explain how cursor distance can trigger an interaction. However, I made the visual and experience decisions myself, including changing the fish idea, adding a start screen, using random seasons and locations, and choosing the music and sounds. One remaining question is how to make the hidden objects look more like a unified illustration instead of simple symbols. In a future version, I would replace the symbols with my own drawings or carefully chosen image assets.
