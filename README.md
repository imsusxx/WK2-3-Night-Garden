# Night Garden

Night Garden is a small interactive web experience about slowing down and paying attention. It was made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

· Background story

After a busy day, a traveller finds a garden that only appears when the world becomes quiet. The garden changes every time it is visited: it may be a forest trail, a moonlit pond, or a hidden grove, and it can appear in a different season or time of day.

The garden is full of small living details—flowers, insects, leaves, stones, and other hidden objects. They do not reveal themselves immediately. The traveller has to move slowly, focus a small light, and spend time noticing what is already there.

· Purpose

This website is designed as a gentle focus and calm-down experience. It encourages the player to slow their cursor movement, look closely at one area at a time, and take a short break from fast or distracting online activity. The soft colors, quiet piano introduction, ambient music, and small bell feedback are meant to make the interaction feel peaceful rather than competitive.

· How to play

1. Open the website and press 'START'.
2. Read the short rules, then press 'BEGIN WALK'.
3. Move the cursor through the garden. The cursor becomes a flashlight.
4. Use the mouse wheel to make the flashlight beam narrower or wider.
5. Hold the light over a hidden object for a moment to discover it.
6. Each walk randomly selects '5–8 objects' from a collection of **25 possible objects**.
7. A bell plays when an object is found. Find every object in the current walk to hear the success sound and unlock 'NEW WALK'.

· Random experience

Every new walk changes the location, season, time of day, hidden objects, object positions, and ambient track. This means the player cannot memorize one map; each visit asks for fresh attention.
